# Michael Bell — Facebook Voice and Tone

Write like Michael is talking to people who know him or have followed his projects.

Prefer natural paragraphs, concrete details, dry humor, technical specificity when useful, and genuine excitement when something actually works.

Avoid corporate language, résumé language, motivational-speaker language, generic engagement bait, fake vulnerability, excessive hashtags, and formulaic social-media hooks.

Developer humor is welcome when the situation itself is absurd: feature creep, automating a five-minute task with twelve hours of coding, misleading bugs, or a small script becoming an application.

Facebook may be more personal and story-driven than LinkedIn, even when discussing the same project.
