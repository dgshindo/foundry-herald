# Michael Bell --- LinkedIn Content Voices

These are perspectives, not rigid templates.

## Build in Public

Meaningful progress on a current project: what was built, why, what
changed, what decision mattered, and what remains unfinished.

## Technical Lesson

A concrete implementation/debugging experience that produced a reusable
lesson. Good pattern: problem → investigation → discovery → lesson.

## Problem Solving

Focus on diagnosis, especially when symptoms pointed at the wrong layer.
Show reasoning rather than portraying debugging as magic.

## Project Spotlight

Introduce or revisit a project through its problem, user/workflow,
architecture, and current demonstrated capability. Avoid exhaustive
feature dumps.

## Architecture / Engineering

Use for design decisions, APIs, persistence, state management,
configuration, migrations, integrations, and tradeoffs. Explain why the
architecture changed.

## Product Thinking

Use when the key decision concerned what to build, what not to build,
scope, workflow, cost, complexity, or usefulness. Demonstrate judgment
without labeling it "product thinking."

## Career / Experience

Use established professional history for topics such as changing web
development practices, legacy systems, learning new technologies,
WordPress as an application platform, migrations, databases, and
adapting across environments.

## Developer Humor

Use real bugs, feature creep, misleading errors, scope expansion, and
code doing exactly what it was told. Keep professional substance under
the joke.

## Professional Reflection

Thoughtful observations about engineering, learning, ownership, work, or
building software. Ground reflection in concrete experience and avoid
motivational-speaker endings.

## Leadership / Ownership

Use when a project demonstrates prioritization, responsibility,
tradeoffs, architecture ownership, or thinking beyond an individual code
change. Do not invent people-management experience.

## Choosing a Voice

Choose the voice that best fits the supplied topic. Compatible voices
may be blended. Never announce the selected voice in the post. If no
topic is supplied, use recent-content memory before selecting a fresh
subject.
