# Michael Bell --- LinkedIn Content Rules

## Purpose

Create professional content demonstrating real experience, technical
reasoning, current work, learning, and judgment.

## Accuracy

Never invent employers, titles, dates, clients, outcomes, revenue,
metrics, user counts, team sizes, responsibilities, certifications,
degrees, product availability, testimonials, leadership
responsibilities, or technologies.

Distinguish clearly between idea, planned feature, experiment, work in
progress, working feature, and tested capability.

## Show, Don't Claim

Prefer evidence over adjectives. Describe the problem and diagnosis
instead of saying "strong problem solver." Explain an architectural
tradeoff instead of claiming architecture expertise.

## One Post, One Main Idea

Do not cram an entire project's architecture into every post. A Herald
post might focus only on brand isolation, a state bug, configuration, or
why a feature was postponed.

## Repetition

Use recent-content memory to avoid repeating the same anecdote, hook,
lesson, conclusion, scope-creep joke, or career message.

## Professional Relevance

Favor software development, engineering, architecture, debugging,
product development, technical leadership, career experience, learning,
projects, technology, and professional decision-making.

## Calls to Action

Do not mechanically end with "Thoughts?", "Agree?", or "What do you
think?" Ask a question only when a specific answer would create useful
discussion.

## Promotion

Projects may be discussed proudly without every post becoming an
advertisement.

## Political Content

This profile is primarily professional. Do not import unrelated Facebook
political arguments or culture-war material unless explicitly requested
and professionally relevant.

## AI / Tooling

Do not force AI into posts merely because a project uses AI-related
technology. When Michael explicitly asks to leave AI/LLMs out, respect
that boundary completely.

## Platform Fit

Favor authentic professional experience, useful insight, concrete
observations, and original perspective. Avoid generic recycled advice
and engagement bait.

## Publishing Rule

Herald does not publish to Michael's LinkedIn profile. After approval,
the only LinkedIn action is Copy to Clipboard. Michael manually pastes
and publishes the final post.
